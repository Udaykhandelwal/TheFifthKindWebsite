How much does social media management cost in India in 2026?

By Uday Khandelwal, The Fifth Kind · Published 20 November 2026

Short answerIn India in 2026, most small businesses pay ₹15,000–₹50,000 a month for social media management from a freelancer or small agency, and ₹50,000–₹1,50,000 a month for a fuller agency service that includes strategy, video production and ads management. Large multi-platform brands can pay ₹1 lakh to ₹5 lakh or more. Ad spend paid to Meta is extra in every case.

This guide is for owners and marketing managers deciding whether to hire a freelancer, an agency or someone in-house, and who want to know what a fair monthly fee buys.

These are planning ranges compiled from published Indian agency rate guides. Industry matters too: one benchmark puts e-commerce brands at about ₹40,000–₹70,000 a month and B2B companies at about ₹50,000–₹80,000.

What a monthly fee should include

  • Strategy: who you're talking to, content pillars, tone, and one clear goal each month.
  • A content calendar you approve before anything is posted.
  • Production: reels, carousels, static posts and stories. Check how many of each, and who shoots or creates the video.
  • Posting and community: scheduled posts, and replies to comments and DMs within a set time.
  • Ads management if you run Meta ads. The fee for managing ads is separate from the ad spend itself.
  • A monthly report that explains reach, saves, shares, followers and enquiries, and what changes next month.

What changes the price

  1. Video volume. Reels cost far more to make than graphics. Five good reels a month is a very different job from five templated posts.
  2. Who makes the video. Agencies that shoot, edit or produce AI video in-house charge more but keep quality consistent.
  3. Number of platforms. Instagram alone is cheaper than Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube together.
  4. Community management. Daily replies to comments and DMs take real hours.
  5. Ads. Campaign setup, testing and reporting add to the fee.
  6. Languages. Content in English plus Hindi, Kannada or another language takes more writing and review.

How to judge value

  • Ask for accounts they actually run, not a showreel. Look at the last 30 posts on those pages.
  • Ask who makes the video and how many reels are in the price.
  • Agree on one number that matters, such as enquiries on WhatsApp or store visits, not only followers.
  • Make sure you own the accounts and the content, and that the ad account is in your business's name.
  • Prefer a 3-month start. One month is rarely enough to see what works.

FAQ

What is a fair price for social media management in India?
For a small business, ₹15,000 to ₹50,000 a month from a freelancer or small agency is common in 2026. A complete agency service with strategy, in-house video and ads management usually costs ₹50,000 to ₹1,50,000 a month. Ad spend is paid separately.
Is ad spend included in the management fee?
Usually not. The agency's fee covers planning, content and management. The money you spend on Instagram or Facebook ads is paid directly to Meta, ideally from an ad account in your business's name.
Should I hire a freelancer or an agency?
A freelancer suits simple posting on a tight budget. An agency suits brands that need regular video, consistent design, ads and someone accountable for results. Many businesses start with an agency for strategy and video, then add in-house help later.
How long before social media management shows results?
Expect the first month to set the strategy and find what works, and clearer results from the second or third month, when the calendar, content style and ads have been tuned to real data.
