Frequently asked questions

Can the AI character look the same across videos?
Yes. We design a character sheet and keep the look consistent across scenes and episodes, which is essential for mascots and series.
How long can a character film be?
Custom length. Most brand character films are 20–60 seconds for social media, and longer stories are possible for YouTube or events.
Do you write the story too?
Yes. Concept, script, character design, voice and music are all included.
