Frequently asked questions

What is AI video production?
AI video production means creating video with generative AI tools for visuals, voices, characters and effects, directed by human writers and editors. It replaces or reduces cameras, sets and crews, which makes production faster and more affordable, especially for short-form ads and product videos.
How long does an AI video take?
Most short AI product videos move from brief to final delivery within a week. Longer brand and ad films take longer depending on script and scope. We confirm the timeline before starting.
Will it look like generic AI content?
No. We start from story and art direction, not a prompt, and every shot is directed, selected and edited by people. Watch our work and judge for yourself.
Do you work with brands outside Bangalore?
Yes. The studio is in Shantinagar, Bengaluru, and we work with brands across India and abroad. Everything from briefing to review happens on WhatsApp and video calls.
Who owns the final video?
You do. You get full usage rights to the final deliverables for your marketing.
