Industries we work with in Chennai

Business areas we know: T. Nagar, OMR, Guindy, Anna Nagar, Nungambakkam and Mylapore.

IT & SaaS companies

Video: Product walkthroughs, launch videos and AI presenters that explain software to buyers who skim.

Social: Thought-leadership reels and carousels that turn product updates into content people share.

Chat: Support and pre-sales chatbots trained on your docs, so first-line questions never wait for a ticket.

Automotive & engineering firms

Video: Product and process films for components and machines that are hard to photograph well.

Social: B2B content and launch campaigns aimed at dealers, buyers and future hires.

Chat: Dealer, spare-part and quotation enquiries answered from your catalogue.

Hospitals, clinics & doctors

Video: Doctor-led explainer reels with an AI clone, so specialists share advice without filming every week.

Social: Trust-building health education content and patient-friendly carousels, within medical advertising norms.

Chat: Appointment booking, timings and 'which doctor should I see' questions handled on WhatsApp.

Jewellers

Video: Macro films of real pieces, lit so gold and stones look as they do in the hand, plus festival campaigns.

Social: Reels, carousels and myth-busting history posts that buyers save, with festival calendars planned weeks ahead.

Chat: A WhatsApp catalogue bot that answers 'is this in 18KT?' and books store visits after hours.

Textile & apparel brands

Video: Fabric and fashion films made from flat-lay photos: drape, movement and a full look without a model shoot.

Social: Collection drops, styling reels and festive lookbooks that keep a catalogue moving.

Chat: Wholesale and retail enquiries, MOQ and catalogue requests answered on WhatsApp.

Schools, colleges & coaching institutes

Video: Admission films and course explainers, including a faculty AI clone that records once and teaches every batch.

Social: Admission-season campaigns, result reels and student stories that parents actually watch.

Chat: Admission enquiries, fee and batch-timing questions answered instantly, with counsellor handover.

Manufacturers & B2B suppliers

Video: Product and process films that show what your factory does, for buyers and distributors who never visit.

Social: B2B content that makes a specialist product feel premium, like the Instagram we run for Mahaveer Papers.

Chat: A catalogue-trained assistant that answers spec questions and sends quotations, like Kora for Mahaveer Papers.
