Reel Editing · Bengaluru

Your footage, edited to hold attention.

The Fifth Kind offers professional reel editing for brands and creators. We take the footage you already have (shoots, events, podcasts, phone videos) and cut it into Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and ads. The edits are built for retention: a strong opening hook, tight pacing, sound design, captions and a clear call to action. No AI generation, just craft.

Frequently asked questions

Do you edit podcasts into reels?
Yes. We pull the strongest moments from long recordings and cut them into short clips with hooks and captions.
Can you handle a monthly volume of reels?
Yes. Many clients send footage in batches and get a set of reels every month. Tell us your volume and we'll plan the schedule.
Can you add AI elements to my footage?
Yes. Reel editing can be combined with AI B-roll, AI clone segments or AI-generated scenes. See AI video production.
