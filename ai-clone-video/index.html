Be on camera every week. Without filming.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI clone video?
An AI clone video is a video in which a digital replica of a real person, trained on their appearance and voice, speaks a new script. It looks and sounds like the person without them being filmed for that video.
Is it legal to make an AI clone of myself in India?
Yes, when the person being cloned gives informed consent and the content isn't misleading. We only clone people who have explicitly consented, and we never create clones of third parties or public figures. See our guide on AI clone videos and consent in India.
How realistic does it look?
Very realistic at social-media viewing sizes, especially for talking-head formats. Watch the examples above; the first one is our founder.
How much filming do I need to do?
One short recording session to create the clone. After that, new videos need only a script.
