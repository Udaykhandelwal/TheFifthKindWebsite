Frequently asked questions

Can I meet your team in Bangalore?
Yes. Our studio is at 529, 8th Cross, Nanjappa Rd, Shantinagar, Bengaluru 560027. Clients are welcome to visit by appointment, and briefs can also happen on WhatsApp or a call.
Can you work in English, Kannada and Hindi?
Yes. The assistant can understand and reply in English, Kannada, Hindi, Tamil and Telugu, and hands over to your team in any language they speak.
How much does AI automation cost in Bangalore?
Pricing is the same wherever you are. It depends on scope: the number of films or posts, length, languages and integrations. We give a fixed quote after a free 15-minute call, with no obligation.
Which Bangalore businesses do you work with?
Mostly tech startups, it & saas companies, d2c brands, real estate developers & brokers and schools, colleges & coaching institutes, along with founders and personal brands.
What should a business automate first?
Usually the most repeated task with clear rules: quotations, invoices and payment reminders, or replies to common enquiries. We start with a free review of how your business runs.
Do I need to change my software?
No. We connect AI agents to the tools you already use, such as Google Sheets, your CRM, WhatsApp and your payment gateway.
