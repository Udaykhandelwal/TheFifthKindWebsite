Why jewellery is different

Gold and diamonds are hard to film. A phone camera flattens the shine, and generic AI makes jewellery that doesn't exist. Buyers notice both. We light and grade every piece so the stones and the metal look the way they do in your hand, keep the real design intact, and write in the language your buyers speak.

Frequently asked questions

What does a jewellery marketing agency do?
A jewellery marketing agency plans and runs a jeweller's social media and advertising: a monthly content calendar, product reels and photos, festival and wedding-season campaigns, posting and replies to enquiries, and Instagram and Facebook ads that bring local buyers into the store or onto WhatsApp.
Do you need to visit my store to shoot?
Not always. We can build reels from your existing product photos and short phone clips, and add AI-assisted macro and lifestyle shots that keep your real designs intact. For owner stories and podcast reels, a short shoot at the store works best.
Will AI change how my jewellery looks?
No. We only use AI around your piece, for light, backgrounds and motion, and check every frame against the real design. If a stone or setting changes, the shot is redone.
Which jewellers have you worked with?
We make reels and campaigns for Megha Jewellers and its collections VS Signature and Velvet Lite 9KT, including Dasara and festive-season films in English and Kannada.
How many posts a month do you make?
A typical jewellery plan is about five reels and four posts a month, plus stories. We fix the exact mix and a monthly fee after a free audit of your current Instagram.
Do you work with jewellers outside Bangalore?
Yes. We work with jewellers across India over WhatsApp and calls, and travel for store shoots when the plan needs one.
