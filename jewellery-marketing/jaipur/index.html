The Jaipur jewellery market

Jaipur is a major gemstone cutting and jewellery centre, from Johari Bazaar to export houses, with kundan, polki and coloured stones that need macro filming to do them justice. Jaipur is one of India's great centres of gemstones, jewellery, block printing and handicrafts, and its makers sell to buyers worldwide. Craft-led films show the skill behind each piece.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have an office in Jaipur?
No. Our only studio is in Bengaluru. We work with Jaipur businesses remotely over WhatsApp, calls and shared folders, and travel to Jaipur for shoots when a project needs one.
Can you work in Hindi, English and Rajasthani?
Yes. We work in Hindi, English and Rajasthani. Scripts, captions and voiceovers are written for each language, not translated word for word.
How much does jewellery marketing cost in Jaipur?
Pricing is the same wherever you are. It depends on scope: the number of films or posts, length, languages and integrations. We give a fixed quote after a free 15-minute call, with no obligation.
Which Jaipur businesses do you work with?
Mostly jewellers, handicraft & handloom makers, textile & apparel brands, travel & tourism businesses and hotels & hospitality, along with founders and personal brands.
Which jewellers have you worked with?
Megha Jewellers and its collections VS Signature and Velvet Lite 9KT, with more than 25 approved reels, carousels and festival campaigns in English and Kannada since September 2026.
Will AI change how my jewellery looks?
No. AI is used only around the piece, for light, backgrounds and motion, and every frame is checked against the real design.
