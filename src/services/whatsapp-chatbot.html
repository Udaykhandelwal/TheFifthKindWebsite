Frequently asked questions

What is a WhatsApp chatbot for business?
A WhatsApp chatbot is software connected to a business's WhatsApp number through the official WhatsApp Business API. It replies to customer messages automatically. An AI WhatsApp chatbot understands free-text questions and answers from the business's own product and policy information, instead of offering only fixed menu buttons.
Can I use my existing business number?
Usually yes. A number can be moved to the WhatsApp Business API, but it can't be used in the regular WhatsApp Business app at the same time. We guide you through the migration, or you can set up a new number just for the bot.
How much does a WhatsApp chatbot cost in India?
There are two parts. First, Meta's per-message charges (for example ₹0.8631 per marketing message and ₹0.115 per utility message in India from October 2026, plus GST). Second, the cost of building and running the bot, which depends on its features and integrations. We give a fixed quote after a free consult.
Will my account get banned for using a bot?
Not if it runs on the official WhatsApp Business API and follows Meta's policies: opted-in contacts, approved templates, and no spam. Unofficial "WhatsApp automation" tools that control a personal WhatsApp account are what get numbers banned. We only build on the official API.
Can the bot hand over to a human?
Yes. When a customer asks for a person, or when the bot spots a high-value lead, it transfers the chat to your team's shared inbox with the full history.
Does it work in Hindi?
Yes. The AI can understand and reply in English, Hindi and Hinglish.
