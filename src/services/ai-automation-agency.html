Frequently asked questions

What is an AI automation agency?
An AI automation agency designs and builds systems that use AI agents and workflow tools to do repetitive business tasks automatically, such as writing quotations, sending invoices, following up on leads and answering customers. The Fifth Kind is an AI automation agency based in Bengaluru, working with businesses across India.
What is an AI agent for business?
An AI agent is software that can read information (an email, a WhatsApp message, a spreadsheet), decide what to do using rules and AI, and take an action such as creating a quotation, updating a record or sending a message. Unlike simple automation, it can handle messy, human-written inputs.
Can you automate quotations and invoices for my business?
Yes. Quotation and invoice automation are two of our most requested builds. The agent uses your own rate sheet, product list and templates, and can require your approval before anything is sent.
Do I need to change my software?
Usually not. We connect to what you already use, like WhatsApp, Google Sheets, Excel, email, Tally, Zoho or your CRM, so your team doesn't have to learn a new system.
Is my business data safe?
We only connect to the systems a workflow needs, use the access permissions you approve, and keep approval steps on anything that goes to a customer if you want them. We walk you through where your data flows before we build anything.
How much does business automation cost?
Pricing depends on the number of workflows and the tools involved. Most projects are a one-time build fee plus a small monthly running cost. The first consult is free, and we quote a fixed price for each workflow before starting.
Is the first consultation really free?
Yes. We spend 15–30 minutes understanding how your business runs and tell you what's worth automating first, even if you decide not to work with us.
