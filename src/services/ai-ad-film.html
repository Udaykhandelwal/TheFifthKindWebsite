Frequently asked questions

Is an AI ad film cheaper than a traditional ad shoot?
Usually by a wide margin, because there's no crew, location, set or equipment rental. Published Indian studio rates put a 30-second AI ad film at roughly ₹75,000–₹1.5 lakh, against around ₹5 lakh for a basic conventional 30-second TVC. Actual cost depends on scope. See our cost guide.
Can AI ad films run on TV and OTT?
Yes. We deliver broadcast-ready masters as well as social cuts. Tell us your media plan and we'll match the specs.
How long does an ad film take?
Longer than a short product video because of scripting and review rounds. We give an exact timeline after the brief.
