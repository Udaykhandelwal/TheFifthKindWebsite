Frequently asked questions

Do you need my physical product?
Usually good photos or 3D files of the product are enough. For products where exact detail matters, like texture or labels, we may ask for a few reference shots so the product stays accurate on screen.
How long does an AI product video take?
A typical 15–45 second AI product video moves from brief to final delivery within a week, including a review round.
Can you make several versions for ads?
Yes. From one concept we can deliver multiple hooks, lengths and aspect ratios for testing on Meta, YouTube and marketplaces.
How much does an AI product video cost?
It depends on length, number of scenes and versions. AI production is typically far cheaper than a studio shoot. See our AI video cost guide for India, or message us for a fixed quote.
