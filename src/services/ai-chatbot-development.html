Frequently asked questions

What is a custom AI chatbot?
A custom AI chatbot is a chatbot trained on one business's own information: its products, prices, specifications, policies and FAQs. It answers customer questions in natural language using only that knowledge. A generic bot either follows fixed menus or gives general internet answers.
Can the chatbot be trained on my product catalogue?
Yes. That's the core of what we build. Product lists, Excel sheets, PDFs, website pages and spec sheets can all be used. When your catalogue changes, we update the chatbot's knowledge.
Will it make things up?
We design the bot to answer only from your approved information. When it doesn't know something, it says so and offers to connect the customer to your team rather than guessing. We test it against your real customer questions before launch.
Can it work on WhatsApp too?
Yes. The same knowledge can power a website chat widget and a WhatsApp chatbot on the official WhatsApp Business API. See WhatsApp chatbot & automation.
Does it support Hindi?
Yes. The chatbot can reply in English, Hindi or Hinglish, usually in whatever language the customer writes in.
How much does a custom AI chatbot cost?
It depends on how much data it needs, how many channels it runs on (website, WhatsApp) and what it connects to (CRM, Google Sheets, calendar). Most costs are a one-time setup plus a small monthly fee for hosting and AI usage. Tell us about your business on WhatsApp and we'll give you a fixed quote after a free 15-minute consult.
Where do the leads go?
Wherever your team works: WhatsApp, email, a Google Sheet or your CRM. Each lead arrives with the customer's details and the full conversation.
