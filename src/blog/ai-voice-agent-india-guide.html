How an AI voice agent works

Every call runs in a fast loop:

  1. Speech-to-text turns what the caller says into text in real time.
  2. The AI brain (a large language model plus your script, rules and data) decides the reply. It can look up information such as today's offer sheet, an order status or free calendar slots.
  3. Text-to-speech speaks the reply in a natural voice.
  4. Actions and logs: after the call, the agent updates your CRM or sheet, sends a WhatsApp follow-up, or alerts a salesperson.

The whole loop needs to feel instant, ideally with under a second of silence, or callers start talking over the agent. Low delay is the main thing that separates a good voice agent from an annoying one.

Hindi, Hinglish and Indian accents

Voice technology has improved a lot for Indian languages, but quality still varies between providers and languages. What to check:

  • Code-switching: real customers mix Hindi and English in one sentence. The agent must understand "kal tak delivery ho jayegi kya?" as easily as English.
  • Numbers and names: rupee amounts, model numbers and brand names must be pronounced correctly. This usually needs tuning.
  • Regional languages: Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi and Bengali support exists but is uneven. Test with real callers before scaling.
  • Noisy lines: test on mobile networks from shops and markets, not just a quiet office.

What drives the cost

  • Setup: script design, voice selection and tuning, integrations (CRM, sheets, WhatsApp, calendar), testing.
  • Per-minute usage: telephony, speech recognition, AI model and voice generation, billed on minutes talked.
  • Volume and call length: 2-minute reminder calls to 5,000 customers cost very differently from 10-minute sales conversations.
  • Integrations: the agent is most useful when it can read live data and write results back.

The fair comparison is against a salaried calling team: its cost per call, its hours, and how consistently it works. An agent doesn't get tired at call 300, but a good human still closes complex deals better. The best setups use AI for volume and humans for closing.

Rules for outbound calling

Outbound commercial calls in India fall under TRAI's regulations on unsolicited commercial communication. In practice:

  • Call people who have a relationship with you or gave consent: your customers, dealers and inbound leads. Don't call purchased lists.
  • Respect Do-Not-Disturb preferences and use properly registered numbers and headers.
  • Say it's an automated assistant if asked, and always offer a human or a callback.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. Check your specific campaign with your telecom provider.

How to start

  1. Pick one high-volume, repetitive call type, like lead callbacks or reminders.
  2. Write down what a successful call looks like and when the agent should hand over.
  3. Pilot on 100–200 calls, listen to the recordings and refine.
  4. Scale up, and then add a second use case.

FAQ

Can an AI calling agent speak Hindi?
Yes. Current voice agents can hold conversations in Hindi and Hinglish, including mixed-language sentences, when tuned properly for Indian accents, product names and rupee amounts.
Can AI voice agents replace my sales team?
Not entirely. They're best at high-volume, repetitive calls: first contact, qualification, reminders, offers and FAQs. Humans still close complex or high-value deals. The best setup has AI doing the volume and passing warm leads to people.
What happens if the customer doesn't pick up?
The agent can retry at a better time and send a WhatsApp message or voice note with the same information, so the message still reaches them.
How long does it take to launch a voice agent?
A focused pilot for one call type can usually be designed, tuned and tested within a few weeks, depending on integrations and approvals.
