How much does an AI video ad cost in India in 2026?

By Uday Khandelwal, The Fifth Kind · Published 21 September 2026

Short answerIn India in 2026, professional AI-led video from an agency typically costs ₹25,000–₹1,20,000 per finished minute. Short social videos cost roughly ₹3,000–₹25,000 each, depending on who makes them. A 30-second AI ad film is commonly quoted at about ₹75,000–₹1.5 lakh, against around ₹5 lakh for a basic traditional 30-second TVC. The difference comes mainly from removing the shoot: crew, cast, location and equipment.

This guide is for founders and marketing managers budgeting for product videos, ads or brand films, who want to know what's a fair price and what you actually get at each level.

AI ad film vs traditional TVC

A traditional ad budget splits roughly 25% pre-production, 50% shoot, 25% post-production. AI production removes most of the shoot half (crew, cast, location, equipment, travel). That's why a serviceable 30-second TVC starts around ₹5 lakh while comparable AI ad films are quoted from about ₹75,000. Adding a regional actor or influencer pushes traditional costs to ₹30–50 lakh.

The second saving is versions. Once the master is approved, extra languages, aspect ratios and cutdowns cost a fraction of a new shoot. That matters a lot for performance marketing, where you want to test many hooks.

AI isn't always the right choice. If the ad depends on a real celebrity, a real location or complex live action with real people, a shoot (or a hybrid) is still better.

What drives the price

  1. Creative direction: a strong concept and script is where most of the value is, and where cheap options cut corners.
  2. Length and number of scenes: every distinct shot needs generation, selection and cleanup.
  3. Product accuracy: keeping a real product's shape, label and colour exact takes more work than a stylised world.
  4. Characters and consistency: recurring characters or clones need design and consistency work.
  5. Sound: professional voiceover, music licensing and sound design.
  6. Versions and rights: number of cutdowns, languages, and where the ad will run.

How to get more for your budget

  • Brief one message and one audience per video.
  • Approve the script and storyboard before production. Changes are cheapest there.
  • Ask for 3 hook variations of the first 3 seconds instead of 3 separate videos.
  • Plan all your aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, 16:9) up front.
  • Look at a studio's real portfolio, not just showreel highlights. Ask what their clients actually ran.

FAQ

Is AI video cheaper than a normal video shoot?
Usually yes, by a wide margin for short ads and product videos, because it removes crew, cast, location and equipment costs. Published Indian rates put a 30-second AI ad film at roughly ₹75,000–₹1.5 lakh versus about ₹5 lakh for a basic 30-second TVC.
How long does an AI video ad take to make?
Short AI product videos typically take days to about a week from brief to delivery. Longer brand films with more review rounds take longer.
Can AI videos be used for Meta and YouTube ads?
Yes. They're well suited to it, because many hook and format variations can be produced from one approved concept for testing. Some platforms ask you to disclose AI-generated realistic content, so follow each platform's labelling rules.
