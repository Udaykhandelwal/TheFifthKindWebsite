V · The fidelity promise

AI builds the world around your piece. It never redraws it.

Generic AI tools quietly simplify a band, reinterpret a setting or invent a stone. In our work the piece comes only from your photograph or CAD file, and every frame is checked against it before you see it.

  • Prongs, setting and metal colour
  • Proportion and true scale on the model
  • Engraving, hallmark and clasp

Questions

Will AI change my design?
No. AI creates the scene, the light and the model. The jewellery itself comes only from your photograph or CAD file, and we check every frame against it before you see it.
Do you need the physical jewellery?
No. Good product photos are enough, and CAD files work too. Your pieces never leave your store or vault.
How fast is it?
A free sample reel in 48 hours. A full collection launch usually takes about ten working days.
Who owns the content?
You do. You get full commercial rights to everything we make for your brand.
Do you work with jewellers outside Bengaluru?
Yes. We work with jewellers across India and abroad, entirely online over WhatsApp, calls and email.
What does it cost?
It depends on how much you need each month. Start with a free reel of one of your pieces, then we'll suggest a plan that fits your calendar.
