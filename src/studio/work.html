Questions about our work

Which brands has The Fifth Kind worked with?

Why are there so few films here?
On purpose. This page only shows work that was approved by the client and published. Drafts, tests and unused versions stay off the site.
Is every film on this page made with AI?
Most of them use generative AI for scenes, characters or voices, directed and finished by our editors. The reel edits cut a client's own footage, and the 3D shorts in our "games made real" series are built in Blender.
Do you make videos in Indian languages?
Yes. We have delivered films in English, Hindi, Hinglish, Kannada, Tamil and Telugu. AI clone videos can repeat the same message in several languages in the speaker's own voice.
