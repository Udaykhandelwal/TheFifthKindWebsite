Example: a daily dealer-offer calling system

This is the kind of system we design for distributors. Take a mobile phone distributor with hundreds of retail dealers. Every morning there are new offers and margins across brands. One salesperson can realistically call 30–40 dealers a day, so most dealers hear about the deal late or never.

  1. The team updates one offer sheet each morning.
  2. The AI agent calls every dealer in natural Hindi or Hinglish. It shares the day's offers, answers basic questions ("how many pieces are available?") and asks whether they want to book.
  3. Missed calls get a WhatsApp voice note and a text with the same offer, so no dealer misses it.
  4. Interested dealers are sent to the owner's WhatsApp with name, brand and requirement, ready for a salesperson to close.
"Namaste bhai, aaj ke offers mein is model pe achha margin hai. Aapke liye book kar dun?" The sales call your best person would make, made to every dealer every day.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI voice agent?
An AI voice agent is software that holds spoken phone conversations. It uses speech recognition to understand the caller, an AI model to decide what to say, and a natural-sounding synthetic voice to reply. It can answer inbound calls, make outbound calls, and log the results of every conversation.
Can an AI voice agent speak Hindi?
Yes. We build voice agents that speak Hindi, Hinglish and English, and we tune them for Indian accents, product names and rupee amounts. More Indian languages depend on the voice technology available for that language; ask us about yours.
Will customers know they're talking to AI?
Modern voices sound very natural, but we recommend the agent is transparent that it's an AI assistant when asked. That's good practice and builds trust. What matters most to callers is getting a quick, correct answer.
What happens when a call gets complicated?
The agent transfers the call to a person or schedules a callback, and sends your team a summary of the conversation so the customer doesn't have to repeat themselves.
What does an AI calling agent cost?
Usually a one-time setup (script, voice, integrations) plus usage-based running costs per minute of calling. The total depends on call volume and integrations. We give a fixed setup quote after a free consult. Read our AI voice agent guide for Indian businesses.
Is outbound AI calling allowed in India?
Calls must follow TRAI's telecom commercial communication rules: respect DND preferences, only call people you have a relationship with or consent from, and use proper registered numbers. We design campaigns around consented contact lists such as your customers, dealers and inbound leads.
