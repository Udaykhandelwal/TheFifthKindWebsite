# the5thkind.in — SEO, GEO and Google Ads system

Built 20 November 2026. Companion files in this folder: `KEYWORDS.md` (keyword map for every service), `GOOGLE_ADS_BANGALORE.md` (ad groups, keywords, negatives, landing pages), `PAGES.md` (every page with title and word count), `autocomplete.json` (raw keyword data).

Nothing below is live until the site is pushed. Rankings are earned over months; this work gives every service and city a page that deserves to rank, and removes the technical reasons it couldn't.

## 3. Keyword research

Method: Google autocomplete for India across 11 services, with 7 modifiers each (about 1,250 real search phrases), plus SERP review of the main commercial queries. Full map with primary, secondary, long-tail, transactional, commercial, informational, question, local and low-competition keywords: **`KEYWORDS.md`**.

What the data says:
- Real city demand exists for: "AI video production company in Hyderabad / Delhi / Kochi / Mumbai / Bangalore", "AI automation agency in Bangalore / Mumbai / Pune / Delhi / Chennai / Ahmedabad", "AI chatbot development company in Bangalore", "jewellery digital marketing agency in Jaipur", "jewellery marketing agency Mumbai", "ad film makers in Bangalore", "3D animation studio in Bangalore", "video editing services in Bangalore". The city pages were chosen from this, not guessed.
- Price questions are common: "social media management cost in India", "WhatsApp chatbot price in India", "AI chatbot development cost in India 2026", "Instagram reels editing charges in India". These are answered in FAQs and guides.
- Most "AI clone/avatar/product video" searches are people looking for **free DIY tools** ("generator free"). They are not our buyers and are excluded (and listed as Ads negatives).
- Limitation: autocomplete has no search volumes. Keyword Planner volumes need a Google Ads account (Pending).

## 5. Location architecture

- URL pattern: `/<service>/<city>/` (e.g. `/ai-clone-video/mumbai/`), with the existing service page as the parent, plus `/locations/<city>/` overviews and the `/locations/` index with a service × city matrix. The existing service URLs were kept, so no ranking history is lost; `/services/<service>/<city>` redirects to the new pattern.
- Pages: **59 service-city pages + 20 city overviews + /locations/ = 80 new pages.**
  - Bengaluru: 10 services (our studio city and the Google Ads market).
  - Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune: AI video, AI clone, AI product video, social media, AI chatbot, WhatsApp chatbot, AI automation.
  - Jewellery marketing in 11 jewellery centres: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Jaipur, Surat, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Coimbatore, Kochi.
  - AI video in Kochi, Ahmedabad, Kolkata; AI automation in Ahmedabad (autocomplete demand).
  - The other 14 cities get one rich overview page each, not 11 thin pages.
- Not doorway pages: each page combines the service with that city's industries, business areas, languages, festival and buying seasons, and city-specific FAQs. Measured 5-word-shingle overlap between pages of the same service: **average 39%, maximum 58%** (checked by `tools/seo_check.py`, which fails the build check above 60%).
- Honesty rules built into the generator: only Bengaluru is described as having a studio (with the real address). Every other city page says plainly that we work remotely and travel for shoots. No invented reviews, numbers or addresses.
- Schema: `Service` with `areaServed: City` (and state), `availableLanguage`, `provider` → the Bengaluru business; `Place` on city overviews; `BreadcrumbList` Home › Service › City.

## 7. Google Ads readiness (Bangalore first)

Full plan: **`GOOGLE_ADS_BANGALORE.md`** (11 ad groups, exact and phrase keywords, shared negatives, landing page per ad group, conversion actions).
- Each Bangalore landing page: H1 matching the ad group, WhatsApp and call buttons above the fold, quote form (`#quote`), proof work, price/office/language FAQs, mobile sticky WhatsApp/Call bar.
- Conversions tracked on every page: `generate_lead` (form), `whatsapp_click`, `phone_click`, `email_click`, plus `video_play`. Each event carries `service`, `city` and `page_type`, so GA4 and Ads can report by service and city.
- SEO pages and paid ads are separate systems: campaigns, bids, budgets, targeting and ad approvals are configured in Google Ads.

### How to add a service or city later
Add the city to `data/cities.json` (facts only, no address unless you open an office) or a service to `data/services.json`, list cities under the service's `cities`, then `python3 tools/build.py && python3 tools/seo_check.py && python3 tools/seo_report.py`, commit, push, `python3 tools/indexnow.py`.
