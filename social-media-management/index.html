Social Media Management · Bengaluru, India

We run your social media. You run the business.

The Fifth Kind is a social media management agency in Bengaluru that runs Instagram and other social channels end to end for brands in India and abroad. Every month we set the strategy, plan a content calendar you approve, produce the reels, films and carousels in-house, post them, answer comments and DMs, run Meta ads and send a report on what it got you. We currently run the Instagram of Mahaveer Papers, a Bengaluru speciality paper house, and our own page, where one reel reached 885,000 people.

Everything a page needs, from one team.

Most brands juggle a freelancer for posts, an editor for reels and someone else for ads. The quality drops and nobody owns the result. We do all of it, and the films are made by the same studio that makes ads for Arvind Mills and Schüco.

Strategy

Audience and competitor study, content pillars, tone of voice, and one clear goal per month: reach, followers, enquiries or sales.

Monthly content calendar

Every reel, carousel and story planned with hooks, captions and hashtags. You approve the month before anything is posted.

Content production

AI product films, brand reels, AI clone videos, carousels and edits of your own footage, all made in-house in your brand's look.

Posting & community

Scheduled posting, replies to comments and DMs, and comment-to-DM keyword flows that turn viewers into leads on WhatsApp.

Meta ads

We boost the posts that already work and run Instagram and Facebook campaigns to the right audience, on a budget you set.

Reporting

Reach, followers, saves, shares and leads each month, with what we learned and what changes next month.

How a month works

  1. Day 1: plan

    We share the month's strategy and calendar. You approve or change it on WhatsApp.

  2. Week 1: produce

    Reels, films and carousels are made and sent for a quick review.

  3. Every week: publish

    Posts go out at the times your audience is active, with captions written for search.

  4. Every day: reply

    Comments and DMs are answered. Serious enquiries are passed to you on WhatsApp.

  5. Month end: report

    A plain report of what grew, what didn't and what we change next month.

Frequently asked questions

What does social media management include?
At The Fifth Kind it includes strategy, a monthly content calendar, content production (reels, AI films, carousels, stories and captions), posting, replies to comments and DMs, Meta ads management and a monthly performance report.
Which platforms do you manage?
Mostly Instagram, along with Facebook and LinkedIn. Reels are also cut for YouTube Shorts when it helps the brand.
Do I need to shoot content myself?
No. Much of the content is made with AI: product films, AI clone videos of the founder and motion graphics. If you have your own footage or products to film, we edit that too.
Do I approve posts before they go live?
Yes. You approve the monthly calendar first, and every reel is sent for review before it is posted.
How much does social media management cost?
It's a monthly retainer that depends on how many posts and reels you need, whether ads are included and how many platforms we run. We quote a fixed monthly fee after a free audit of your current page. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.
Which brands do you manage social media for?
We run the Instagram of Mahaveer Papers, a Bengaluru speciality paper company, social content and Meta ads for CAPTÉ in France, and our own page @the5thkind.ai.
Are you based in Bangalore?
Yes, our studio is in Shantinagar, Bengaluru. We work with brands across India and abroad over WhatsApp and calls.
