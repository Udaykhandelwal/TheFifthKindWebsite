How Chennai businesses use AI product videos

Chennai is home to many of India's SaaS companies, a large automotive belt and some of the busiest jewellery and silk streets in the country. Tamil-first content performs strongly here.

IT & SaaS companies

Product walkthroughs, launch videos and AI presenters that explain software to buyers who skim.

Automotive & engineering firms

Hospitals, clinics & doctors

Jewellers

Macro films of real pieces, lit so gold and stones look as they do in the hand, plus festival campaigns.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have an office in Chennai?
No. Our only studio is in Bengaluru. We work with Chennai businesses remotely over WhatsApp, calls and shared folders, and travel to Chennai for shoots when a project needs one.
Can you work in Tamil and English?
Yes. We work in Tamil and English. Scripts, captions and voiceovers are written for each language, not translated word for word.
How much do AI product videos cost in Chennai?
Pricing is the same wherever you are. It depends on scope: the number of films or posts, length, languages and integrations. We give a fixed quote after a free 15-minute call, with no obligation.
Which Chennai businesses do you work with?
Mostly it & saas companies, automotive & engineering firms, hospitals, clinics & doctors, jewellers and textile & apparel brands, along with founders and personal brands.
Do you need my product physically?
Usually not. Clear photos of the product from a few angles are enough. For complex products we may ask for a sample or a short phone video.
Can I use these videos in ads?
Yes. They are made for Meta, YouTube and website use, with the formats and hook variations ads need.
